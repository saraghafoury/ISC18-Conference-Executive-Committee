# 18th Iranian Statistical Conference — Executive Committee

This repository contains selected reports, presentations, posters, and visual materials prepared during my involvement as a member of the **Executive Committee of the 18th Iranian Statistical Conference**, hosted by **K. N. Toosi University of Technology (KNTU)**.

## 📌 About the Conference

The 18th Iranian Statistical Conference brought together researchers, university professors, students, and professionals from various fields related to statistics, data, and computational sciences.

As a member of the executive committee, I had the opportunity to contribute to the organization and preparation of materials for the conference.

## 👩🏻‍💻 My Contributions

My contributions included:

* Preparing reports and presentations related to the conference
* Organizing and presenting conference-related information
* Preparing posters and visual materials
* Supporting the executive team during the conference
* Coordinating and assisting with conference activities
* Working with faculty members, researchers, and other members of the executive committee
